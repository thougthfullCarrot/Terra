    Questions

    Good to know

    Why do I need an account?

    Signing in confirms your email and keeps your saved jobs, tracker and profile private to you. It's free and needs only an email address.

    Where do the jobs come from?

    From each firm's own Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday and iCIMS boards, plus a few listings from Adzuna. Terra checks them every two hours.

    Who sees my resume?

    Only you. It is stored privately, and matching runs in your browser. No AI service reads it.

    Which cities are covered?

    Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, Austin, San Antonio, El Paso, New Braunfels, College Station, Galveston, Lubbock and Midland. How much each feature has varies by city and by what public sources publish.
