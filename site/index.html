QUESTIONS

Good to know

Where do the jobs come from?

From each firm's own Greenhouse, Lever, Workday and iCIMS boards, plus a few listings from Adzuna. Terra checks them every two hours.

Who sees my resume?

Only you. It is stored privately, and matching runs in your browser. No AI service reads it.

Which cities are covered?

Dallas, Fort Worth, Houston, Austin, San Antonio, El Paso, New Braunfels, College Station, Galveston, Lubbock and Midland.
