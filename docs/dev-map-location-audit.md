# Development Map location audit (PR #101)

Run 2026-10-26 on the live build's data (10,511 projects, 11 markets), read-only, GitHub Actions run 40388328983. Full per-market tables with flagged points and every sample check: [audit-run.md](dev-map-location-audit-run.md).

## Bottom line
- **Confirmed errors in the sample: 0 of 190 checked points.** No sampled pin was proven wrong by an official record (city permit GIS or a validated Census match outside the filed ZIP).
- **Suspected: 15**, listed below. Most are TDLR filings whose address itself is inconsistent (wrong ZIP, N vs S on FM 973, two addresses in one field). One looks genuinely misplaced: Marbach Brake Check, "2328 W Loop 1604 S, 78245", geocoded about 20 km south of its ZIP.
- **The real pipeline bug was a rule, not a sampled pin:** city permits lent their point to state filings on house number + street name alone, ignoring ZIP and town. Rematching all 64 lent points under the new rules: 61 keep the same point, 3 lose it (below).
- Nothing here claims building-level accuracy. Permit and parcel points are address/parcel level; Census geocodes are interpolated along the street.

## Coverage vs accuracy
- 3 filings lose a borrowed point: The Bloom @ Lamar Square (TDLR lists the town as "Travis", the county), DP World (Von Ormy mailing town vs San Antonio permit, no shared ZIP proof) and Construction Contractor Facility Phase 2 (phase name, permit is a sign permit). They become Location not verified until a geocode or a better match places them. That is the price of not lending points on street name alone.
- ZIP-only projects (3,981 across markets, about 38%) are no longer spread into fake site pins; they show as dashed ZIP-area rings and "Location not verified" in the list.
- First audit run taught one fix: refusing every geocode whose ZIP differed from the filing would have thrown away correct points (TDLR files many wrong ZIPs: an Austin site in 78209, Long Creek HS with two ZIPs). Now a same-town match is taken and flagged; a different town and ZIP is refused.

## Not done / limits
- Sample checks only compare against Census and the SA, Austin and Fort Worth permit records; Dallas, Houston, El Paso, Lubbock, Midland, Galveston, College Station and New Braunfels have no city permit GIS in the pipeline, so their samples are Census-only.
- No production data changed, nothing deployed.
